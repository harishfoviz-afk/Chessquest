Brave Pawn

Marches forward 1 square. Captures 1 square diagonally! Reaches the end to become a Queen!

Jumping Knight

Moves in an "L" shape (2 squares then 1). The only piece that can jump over others!

Laser Bishop

Zooms diagonally as far as it wants, staying on its matching square color forever!

Castle Rook

Rolls straight forward, backwards, left, or right as far as the path is open!

Queen Stella

Super powerful! Combines the moves of the Rook and Bishop in any direction!

King Leo
